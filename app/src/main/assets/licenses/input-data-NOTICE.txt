Vime public input vocabulary and associations

Derived solely from fixed Luna Pinyin and Rime Essay sources (LGPL-3.0).
Luna: https://github.com/rime/rime-luna-pinyin/tree/46acf03142c12b5aeed7002675046bf7255eed35
Essay: https://github.com/rime/rime-essay/tree/054920de4f54c9e5994276a96a4fc2a35cb51aa3

Converted to simplified Chinese, preserving reading-weight tiers; compiled by
pinned librime, exported with its unmodified public table decompiler, then
encoded into VIMELEX1 by tools/build_input_lexicon.py. All public word/code rows
are retained. No private user dictionary, legacy Vime input or English gloss is
merged into this data. Modifications: Vime contributors, 2026.

Matching sources, generator and public export are supplied in the accompanying
Vime source archive. input/manifest.json identifies exact inputs and output.
LGPL-3.0 and its complete GPL-3.0 companion are included in this app.
